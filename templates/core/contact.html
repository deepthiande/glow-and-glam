Frequently Asked Questions

What is your delivery timeframe and shipping costs?

We offer complimentary standard tracked shipping on all orders over $50 within the USA and internationally. For orders below $50, flat-rate shipping is $5.00. Express Glam Courier delivery takes 1-2 business days.

Are GLOW & GLAM formulations genuinely 100% cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes, without exception. We are proudly Leaping Bunny certified. We do not test on animals, nor do we sell in territories where animal testing is mandated by law. All products and brush fibers are 100% vegan.

How can I determine my exact foundation shade match?

Each product page includes our interactive shade swatches and undertone breakdown. You can also message our beauty concierge directly for a complimentary digital shade match analysis.

What is your return and satisfaction policy?

We want you to adore your purchase. If you are not completely enchanted with any item, returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or complimentary shade exchange.
